IELTS Speaking Workshop · Band 7–9
TECHNOLOGY

How technology has changed our lives and the way we communicate, work, study, and entertain ourselves.

Speaking Focus

  • Personal experiences with technology
  • Advantages and disadvantages
  • Technology in education and the workplace
  • Artificial Intelligence and future developments
  • Social media and digital communication
Band 8+ Tip

Don't just say: «Technology is useful.»

Instead: «Technology has revolutionized the way we communicate, access information, and carry out everyday tasks.»

Language Bank

Vocabulary, Idioms & Useful Phrases

Vocabulary

1. Artificial Intelligence (AI) — machines that think. «AI helps me study faster.»
2. Automation — machines doing tasks. «Automation has changed factories.»
3. Innovation — a new idea or invention. «Innovation drives economic growth.»
4. Digital literacy — skill in using tech. «Digital literacy is essential today.»
5. Cybersecurity — protection from online attacks. «Cybersecurity is a growing concern.»
6. Cutting-edge technology — the newest tech. «Hospitals use cutting-edge technology.»
7. Technological advancement — progress in tech. «Advancements have transformed education.»
8. Virtual communication — talking online. «Virtual communication saved us during lockdown.»
9. Data privacy — keeping personal data safe. «Data privacy worries many users.»
10. User-friendly — easy to use. «This app is really user-friendly.»
11. Productivity — efficiency in work. «Technology boosts my productivity.»
12. Digital transformation — moving to digital systems. «Digital transformation changed banking.»

Idioms

At the click of a button — very easily and instantly.
«We can access information at the click of a button.»
Keep up with technology — stay informed about new tech.
«It's difficult to keep up with technology.»
A double-edged sword — both advantages and disadvantages.
«Social media is a double-edged sword.»
On the same wavelength — thinking in a similar way.
«Tech keeps us on the same wavelength.»
Light years ahead — far more advanced.
«Some countries are light years ahead.»

Useful IELTS Phrases

There's no denying that...
«...technology plays a crucial role in modern life.»
What stands out to me is...
«...how technology has made communication much faster.»
From my perspective...
«...technological advancement has improved our quality of life.»
To a certain extent...
«...people have become overly dependent on technology.»
One of the main reasons is that...
«...technology saves time and increases productivity.»

Accuracy + Range

Grammar Range: Sound Like a Band 8+ Speaker

1. Present Perfect

«Technology has changed the way we communicate.»

2. Passive Voice

«People are constantly exposed to digital content.»

3. Relative Clauses

«I use several apps that help me stay organized.»

4. Conditionals

«If technology continues to develop at this pace, many jobs will become automated.»

5. Comparatives

«Online communication is far more convenient than traditional communication.»

6. Concession / Contrast

«Although technology has made our lives easier, it has also created new problems.»

7. Cause and Effect

«Because people rely heavily on technology, face-to-face interaction has declined.»

IELTS Tip

Do NOT force complicated grammar.

Range + Accuracy + Naturalness = Better Grammar Score

Answer-Building Technique

Don't Give Short Answers

WHAT→ WHY→ EXAMPLE→ RESULT

Weak Answer

«Yes, I use technology a lot. It's useful.»

Better Answer

«Yes, I use technology quite extensively because it makes my daily routine more efficient. For example, I rely on several apps to organize my work and communicate with colleagues. As a result, I can save a considerable amount of time.»

IELTS Tip

Instead of a short opinion, develop your answer with: Opinion → Reason → Example → Result

Accuracy Clinic

Common IELTS Mistakes

1. Technology vs. Technologies

❌ I love technologies.

✅ I love technology. / I love modern technologies.

Technology = uncountable, general. Technologies = different types or systems.

2. Technology vs. Technique

❌ Learning technology is important for speaking English.

✅ Learning new techniques is important for speaking English.

Technology = tools and systems. Technique = a method or skill.

3. Repetition of "Technology"

❌ Technology is important because technology helps people and technology improves life.

✅ Technology is important because it helps people and improves their quality of life.

Alternatives: innovation · digital tools · technological advancement · modern devices · AI-powered systems

Accuracy Clinic · Part 2

More Common Mistakes

4. Incorrect Preposition

❌ depend to technology

❌ depend of technology

✅ depend on technology

5. Wrong Adjective Form

❌ technology development

✅ technological development

✅ technological advancement

6. Only "Good" or "Bad"

❌ Technology is good.

✅ Technology is highly beneficial.

✅ Technology is a double-edged sword.

✅ Technology has transformed the way we live and work.

Lexical Resource

Upgrade Your IELTS Language

Basic

❌ «Technology is important.»

Band 7+

«Technology plays an important role in modern life.»

Band 8+

«Technology has revolutionized nearly every aspect of modern life.»

Another option: «There's no denying that technological advancement has improved efficiency and connectivity.»

Complex Version

«Although technology offers countless benefits, excessive reliance on it may create new challenges.»

Remember

Don't replace every simple word with a difficult word. Upgrade your language when it sounds natural.

Exam Strategy

IELTS Technology: Band 7–9 Tips

1. Don't memorize full answers.

Memorize useful language, not complete responses.

2. Develop your ideas.

Don't stop after giving your opinion.

3. Use vocabulary naturally.

One well-used advanced word beats five forced words.

4. Show grammatical range.

Mix simple and complex structures.

5. Give examples.

Examples make your ideas clearer and more convincing.

6. Avoid repetition.

Use pronouns, synonyms, and paraphrasing.

7. Don't force idioms.

Use them only when they fit naturally.

8. Answer the question directly.

Especially in Part 3, make your position clear.
